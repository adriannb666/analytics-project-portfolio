# Microbrewery Marketing and Scenario Analysis

**AD715 team project | Marketing contribution**

A business-simulation case for a restaurant microbrewery with eight beer products, retail/wholesale channels, and a 36-month planning horizon.

The marketing work develops differentiated retail prices, annual product allocations, and monthly seasonal demand assumptions. It also distinguishes modeled demand from feasible production and interprets how pricing, volume, capacity, and full operating costs interact.

## Analytical contribution

- Replace flat retail prices with a proposed product-specific scenario.
- Make within-retail allocations explicit and reconcile annual shares to 100%.
- Replace a rounded monthly pattern totaling 99.6% with an annual pattern totaling 100%.
- Preserve earlier simulation cycles and compare scenarios.
- Separate a price-only arithmetic illustration from a full simulation forecast.
- Identify follow-up tests for price response, supply allocation, wholesale pricing, and cost completeness.

Prices and seasonal percentages are planning assumptions rather than measured elasticity or validated customer demand. Several inputs changed together, so observed simulation differences do not isolate a causal price effect. B-SIM's break-even timing is not automatically investment payback.

This public page summarizes the contributor's methods. Shared team reports, project identifiers, live account access, and simulation financial files are not distributed. No standalone original source-code file has been recovered for this browser-based work.
