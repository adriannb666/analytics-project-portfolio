## Coin Metrics data

`projects/crypto-market-sql/data/daily_metrics.csv` is derived from [Coin Metrics Community Data](https://github.com/coinmetrics/data) by Coin Metrics, Inc., licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Only selected columns for five assets were kept, and they were reshaped into one long table. It is used here for non-commercial portfolio purposes.
