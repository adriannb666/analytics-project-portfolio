\section{Non-Technical Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Psychological Factors:}  
    The strategy assumes rational decision-making and perfect execution. In practice, investor emotions such as fear, greed, or panic can lead to deviations from the strategy, impacting performance.

    \item \textbf{Regulatory Changes:}  
    Changes in financial regulations, such as restrictions on cryptocurrency trading or new tax policies, could impact the effectiveness of the strategy, especially for Bitcoin.

    \item \textbf{Market Accessibility:}  
    The ability to execute trades depends on access to trading platforms and markets. Technical issues, platform outages, or geographic restrictions can hinder timely execution.

    \item \textbf{Data Availability and Quality:}  
    The strategy relies on accurate and up-to-date data. Inconsistent data sources or delays in data availability could affect decision-making and performance.

    \item \textbf{Public Perception and Market Sentiment:}  
    The prices of assets like Bitcoin are highly influenced by public opinion and market sentiment. Sudden shifts in perception can cause price volatility that is difficult to predict or respond to effectively.

\end{enumerate}
