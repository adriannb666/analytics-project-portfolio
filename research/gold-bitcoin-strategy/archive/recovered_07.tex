\section{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Dependence on Historical Data:}  
    The strategy relies on historical price data, assuming past trends will continue. Unexpected market events could lead to deviations from these patterns.

    \item \textbf{Simplified Risk Assumptions:}  
    The model assumes returns follow a normal distribution and that gold and Bitcoin are independent. In reality, returns may exhibit fat tails, and correlations can change during market stress.

    \item \textbf{Fixed EMA Parameters:}  
    Static EMA windows and thresholds may limit the strategy's adaptability. Dynamic adjustment of these parameters could improve performance in varying market conditions.

    \item \textbf{Transaction Costs:}  
    While trading fees are considered, additional costs like slippage and market impact are not, which could reduce real-world profits.

    \item \textbf{Limited Asset Scope:}  
    The portfolio includes only gold and Bitcoin. Adding other assets (e.g., stocks or bonds) could enhance diversification and reduce overall risk.

\end{enumerate}
